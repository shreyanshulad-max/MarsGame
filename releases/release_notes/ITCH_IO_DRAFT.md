## Title

MARSMISSION

## Short summary

Explore a Mars research outpost, restore critical systems, study real-world inspired NASA data, and rescue a stranded rover.

## Genre

Educational · Exploration · Puzzle · Simulation

## Player objective

Survive the campaign by completing three sequential missions: fix communications, restore power, and rescue the rover. Optional research earns points for supplies.

## Three missions

1. Communication Blackout  
2. Shelter Power Emergency  
3. Rover Rescue  

## Controls

WASD/Arrows move · E interact · I inventory · J journal · mouse for menus
